\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
In this paper, we introduced Semantic-Balanced Group Relative Policy Optimization (SB-GRPO), a reinforcement learning framework designed to overcome the critical bottlenecks of advantage vanishing and correction bias in LLM reasoning. By synthesizing a Heuristic Confidence Gate with Semantic Error Profiling and an Information-Theoretic Loss Scaling factor, SB-GRPO dynamically stabilizes gradients across highly skewed batches while actively targeting systematic logical flaws. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that this architecture achieves competitive performance on complex mathematical benchmarks, achieving 10.00\% on AIME 2024 and yielding substantial improvements on the MATH-500 dataset under strict hardware and parameter constraints (4-bit QLoRA \cite{hu2021loralowrankadaptationlarge,dettmers2023qloraefficientfinetuningquantized}). 

Notably, SB-GRPO establishes an efficient paradigm for aligning reasoning models, demonstrating that targeted semantic error curation surpasses unguided reflection. Sub-category analysis highlights that SB-GRPO excels in high-complexity reasoning, specifically in \textit{Precalculus} and \textit{Intermediate Algebra}, significantly outperforming existing baselines. Future work will explore extending this entropy-aware balancing mechanism to multi-modal reasoning tasks and evaluating its cross-architecture generalizability across diverse foundation model backbones beyond Qwen2.5-7B.
