\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
In this paper, we introduced Semantic-Balanced Group Relative Policy Optimization (SB-GRPO), addressing advantage vanishing and correction bias in LLM reasoning. By synthesizing a Heuristic Confidence Gate with Semantic Error Profiling and an Information-Theoretic Loss Scaling factor, SB-GRPO stabilizes gradients across skewed batches while actively targeting systematic logical flaws. Under strict 4-bit QLoRA constraints on a single H100 GPU, SB-GRPO achieves 74.80\% on MATH-500 (+2.20\% over M-GRPO) and 10.00\% on AIME 2024, excelling in multi-stage domains like Precalculus (+5.68\%) and Intermediate Algebra (+4.88\%). Future work will explore extending this entropy-aware mechanism to multi-modal reasoning and evaluating cross-architecture generalizability beyond Qwen2.5-7B.
